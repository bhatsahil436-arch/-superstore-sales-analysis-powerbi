# Superstore Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

An interactive 6-page Power BI dashboard analyzing sales, profit and discount
behavior for a retail superstore (2014-2017).

## Key Metrics
- Total Sales: 2.30M
- Total Profit: 286.41K
- Profit Margin: 12.47%
- Total Orders: 5,009
- Avg Order Value: 458.56

## Key Findings
- **Loss-making products:** Tables (-17.7K), Bookcases (-3.5K) and Supplies (-1.2K) lose money.
- **Discounts hurt:** Profit turns negative once discounts exceed 20%, wiping out 135K.
- **Category gap:** Furniture contributes 32% of sales but only 6% of profit (2.5% margin).
- **Regions:** West and East generate ~70% of profit; Central has the lowest margin (7.9%).
- **Operations:** Standard Class carries ~60% of orders and takes ~5 days to deliver.
- **Customers:** 98.5% are repeat buyers; HIGH-value customers drive 39.7% of sales.

## Recommendations
1. Cap discounts at 20%.
2. Reprice or review Tables, Bookcases and Supplies.
3. Investigate loss-making states (Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Illinois).
4. Grow Technology and Office Supplies, which have the best margins.
